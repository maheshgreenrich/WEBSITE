What is Composting
Learn about the process of composting and its positive environmental impact.
1
Step 1: Gather Organic Waste
Start by collecting organic waste such as fruit and vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, and eggshells.
2
Step 2: Create a Compost Pile
Layer the organic waste with brown materials like leaves or shredded paper to create a compost pile.
3
Step 3: Turn and Water the Pile
Regularly turn the compost pile to aerate it and add water to keep it moist for decomposition.
4
Step 4: Use Compost in Your Garden
After a few weeks to months, your compost will be ready to use as nutrient-rich soil for your garden.

Composting Guide
A step-by-step guide on how to start composting at home, including tips and tricks for successful composting.
1
Step 1: Choose a Composting Method
Decide whether you want to compost using a bin, pile, or tumbler. Each method has its own benefits and requirements.
2
Step 2: Select a Location
Find a suitable spot in your backyard or garden for your compost pile. It should be well-drained and easily accessible.
3
Step 3: Gather Composting Materials
Collect a mix of green (nitrogen-rich) and brown (carbon-rich) materials such as kitchen scraps, yard waste, and shredded paper.
4
Step 4: Start Composting
Layer your compost materials, keeping a good balance of greens and browns. Turn the pile regularly to aerate it and speed up the decomposition process.
5
Step 5: Maintain and Troubleshoot
Monitor the moisture and temperature of your compost pile. Troubleshoot any issues like bad odors or slow decomposition by adjusting the mix of materials.
